DotTalk Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to DotTalk. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how DotTalk collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

By using DotTalk, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use a DotTalk account, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address or authentication information
  • Profile information
  • Profile picture, if provided
  • User ID

Messages and Chat Data

When you communicate with other users through DotTalk, we may process:

  • Messages you send and receive
  • Conversation information
  • Information related to your chats and contacts

Device Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Push notification token
  • Basic diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide messaging and communication features
  • Connect you with your friends
  • Deliver notifications
  • Maintain and improve DotTalk
  • Protect against unauthorized access and abuse
  • Fix technical problems
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. Messages and Conversations

DotTalk processes messages so that they can be delivered between users.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information through chats.

4. Notifications

If you enable notifications, DotTalk may use a device notification token to send notifications such as new message alerts.

You can disable notifications through your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

DotTalk may use trusted third-party services to provide certain functionality, including:

  • Authentication services
  • Cloud database and storage services
  • Push notification services
  • Hosting and server infrastructure
  • Analytics or crash reporting services, if enabled

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide DotTalk's services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it where appropriate.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your DotTalk account and associated personal information.

To request account or data deletion, contact us using the contact information provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

DotTalk is not intended for children below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by law.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Accessing your personal information
  • Correcting inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of your information
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing
  • Withdrawing consent where applicable

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the Last Updated date shown at the top of this page.
